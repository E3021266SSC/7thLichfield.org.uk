Scouts

Welcome to Scouts!

Scouts is the fourth section of the Scout Movement, welcoming boys and girls aged 6 to 8 years old. It's all about making friends, having fun and discovering new things through exciting activities and adventures.

At Scouts, there's always something to get involved in! From playing games and learning new skills to going on visits and helping others, our programme gives young people the opportunity to try new things and explore the world around them.

Scouts are encouraged to work together, support one another and develop their confidence and independence. They can also work towards a variety of badges and awards, recognising the skills they learn and achievements they make along the way.re young people can make friends, learn new skills and experience everything that Scouting has to offer.


Our Troops

At 7th Lichfield Scout Group, our Scouts section is made up of three Troops: Garrick, Paget and Swinfen.

Each Troop provides a welcoming environment where young people can make friends, develop new skills and enjoy everything that Scouting has to offer.
